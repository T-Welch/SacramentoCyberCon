FAQ

Frequently asked questions

When and where is it?

Date and venue are still being finalized. Register your interest and we'll email you as soon as they're confirmed.

How much will tickets cost?

We aim to keep it affordable, with discounted student tickets. Pricing will be announced with registration.

I've never given a talk. Can I still apply?

Absolutely. Check "This would be my first talk" and we'll pair you with a mentor to help you prepare.

Will the event be accessible?

We're committed to an accessible event. Venue accessibility details will be posted once the venue is confirmed. If you need an accommodation, let us know in the comments field of the sign up form and we'll follow up with you.

What happens to my information?

It's stored in a private spreadsheet only the organizers can access, and used only to plan the conference and contact you about it. We never sell or share it.

Is there a Code of Conduct?

Yes. The conference follows the DC916 Code of Conduct (opens in a new tab).
